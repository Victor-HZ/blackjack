BLACKJACK STUDIO

Practice with clear boundaries.

Blackjack Studio is an educational application for simulated card-counting and blackjack practice. The app provider is identified in the App Store listing.

Training only

Never use the app while gambling. It does not provide permission to use a device in a casino or other live game. Mental counting and electronic assistance can be treated differently by law. Applicable laws and venue rules vary and may restrict or prohibit activities. The lessons are educational information, not individualized legal or financial advice.

No real-money play

Practice points have no monetary value. They cannot be purchased, transferred, withdrawn, or redeemed. Counting and basic strategy do not guarantee a winning outcome or income. The app does not connect to casinos, receive wagers, or pay prizes.
