## Review notes draft

Blackjack Studio is an offline educational simulator. It does not accept a stream of observed casino cards, use a camera, provide a live casino counter, connect to casinos, or support real-money gambling. A static strategy reference lets the learner study hypothetical two-card hands. The practice table uses free resettable points that cannot be purchased or redeemed. Pro is a non-consumable unlock for additional training content and table settings.

No account is required. Free training is available immediately after the two-step introduction. Pro is available from a locked drill or Settings → Explore Pro. Restore purchases is available from Settings and the upgrade screen. Local training records can be deleted in Settings without deleting the App Store entitlement.
