BLACKJACK STUDIO

Your practice stays yours.

Blackjack Studio stores practice results, completed lessons, table state, and preferences on your device. We do not operate an account system, advertising service, analytics service, or cloud database for this app.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases and handles payment information. The app uses Apple's StoreKit to verify your Pro entitlement, restore purchases, and respond to refunds or revocations. Your payment card details are not provided to the app developer. Apple's services have their own privacy terms.

Local records and backups

Training records are saved in the app's local storage. Depending on your device settings, Apple device backups may include these records. There is no app-operated cross-device progress sync. You can export progress through the iOS share sheet or delete local practice data in Settings. Deleting practice data does not remove your Apple purchase history or Pro entitlement. Copies you export or retain in backups are controlled separately by you.

Optional reminders

Reminders use local notifications, only after you choose to enable them. No push-notification server is used. You can disable reminders in the app or iOS Settings.

No tracking or live-game collection

The app does not request tracking permission, camera, microphone, contacts, or location access. It does not record or analyze real-world casino cards. All practice cards are simulated.

Questions

Contact the provider using the developer support link on this app's App Store listing. This policy applies to the app, not to any external support site or Apple's services.
